Plumbers in Tacoma you can actually reach

Brightwater Plumbing Co. is a family-owned plumbing company that has served homeowners in Tacoma, Lakewood, Puyallup and University Place since 2004. Our licensed plumbers repair and replace water heaters, clear blocked drains, fix leaking pipes and respond to plumbing emergencies around the clock.

Every visit starts with a written, upfront price. You approve the work before we begin, and the price does not change once the job has started, even if it takes longer than we expected.

Plumbing services

  • Water heater repair and replacement for gas and electric tank models.
  • Drain cleaning for kitchen sinks, showers, toilets and main sewer lines.
  • Sewer line repair using camera inspection to find the problem first.
  • 24/7 emergency plumbing for burst pipes, overflowing toilets and no hot water.

Why Tacoma homeowners call us

Most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals from neighbours. We answer the phone ourselves, we arrive inside the two-hour window we give you, and we clean up before we leave. Our plumbers carry common parts on the van, so most repairs are finished on the first visit.

We are licensed, bonded and insured in Washington State, and our workmanship is guaranteed for two years.

Service area

We cover Tacoma, Fircrest, Lakewood, University Place, Puyallup, Fife and Gig Harbor. If you are just outside that area, call us and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you quickly.
