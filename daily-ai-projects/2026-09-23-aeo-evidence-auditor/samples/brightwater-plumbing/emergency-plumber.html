24/7 Emergency Plumber in Tacoma

Plumbing emergencies do not wait for business hours, and neither do we. A licensed Brightwater plumber is on call every night, weekend and holiday, and we aim to be at your door within 90 minutes anywhere in Tacoma, Lakewood and University Place.

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

  • A burst or leaking pipe you cannot isolate.
  • A toilet or drain overflowing with sewage.
  • No hot water in a home with young children or elderly residents.
  • A gas smell near your water heater. Leave the house and call your gas utility first.

What to do while you wait

Turn off the main water valve. In most Tacoma homes it is in the crawl space or where the water line enters the house, near the front of the building. Switch off the power or gas to the water heater if the leak is coming from the tank. Move valuables away from the water and take photos for your insurer.

Emergency call-out pricing

Emergency visits between 8pm and 7am, and on public holidays, carry an after-hours call-out fee. We tell you the fee on the phone before we dispatch a plumber, and the repair itself is priced upfront in writing before any work starts.
