About Brightwater Plumbing Co.

Brightwater Plumbing Co. was started in 2004 by Dana Okafor, a master plumber who spent twelve years working for large contractors before deciding that homeowners deserved straight answers and fair prices. The business began with one van and a phone that rang in the kitchen. Today we have eight licensed plumbers and two apprentices, and Dana still answers the phone most mornings.

How we work

We believe the best plumbing visit is the one where nothing surprises you. That means turning up when we said we would, explaining what we found in plain language, giving you a written price before we start, and cleaning up afterwards. It also means telling you when you do not need us, such as when a simple reset or a cheap part will solve the problem.

Training and licensing

Every Brightwater plumber holds a Washington State plumbing certificate. Our apprentices work alongside a journey-level plumber on every job while they complete their training hours. We attend manufacturer training every year so we can service the latest water heaters and fixtures correctly.

In the community

We sponsor two youth soccer teams in Tacoma and donate a water heater replacement every winter to a family nominated by a local charity. We also run a free workshop each autumn showing homeowners how to find their main shut-off valve and protect their pipes from freezing.
