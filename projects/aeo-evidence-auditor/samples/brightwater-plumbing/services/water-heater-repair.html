Water Heater Repair and Replacement

No hot water, lukewarm showers or a puddle under the tank? Our plumbers diagnose and repair gas and electric tank water heaters from every major manufacturer, including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White and State.

Common water heater problems we fix

  • Pilot lights that will not stay lit and faulty thermocouples on gas models.
  • Burned-out heating elements and failed thermostats on electric models.
  • Leaking temperature and pressure relief valves.
  • Sediment build-up that reduces capacity and makes the tank rumble.
  • Corroded anode rods that shorten the life of the tank.

Repair or replace?

A tank water heater usually lasts 8 to 12 years. If your unit is younger than that and the tank itself is not leaking, a repair is almost always the better value. If the tank is leaking from the bottom, it cannot be repaired and needs to be replaced. We will show you what we found and give you a written price for both options so you can decide.

Water heater replacement

We replace 40, 50 and 75 gallon gas and electric tank water heaters, including the expansion tank, seismic strapping and permit required in Tacoma. Most replacements are completed the same day, and we haul away the old tank.

Maintenance

Flushing the tank once a year removes sediment, keeps the heater efficient and extends its life. We can add a flush to any service visit for a small additional charge.
